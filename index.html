Employee Management

This app is an Employee Management System designed to manage employee details in a simple and digital way. Instead of handling records manually on paper or maintaining large files, everything is stored and managed online in one place. With this system, you can easily register new employees, view all employee information on the dashboard, update their details whenever required, and remove records that are no longer needed. All these tasks can be done quickly with just a few clicks. The main goal of this app is to make employee management faster, easier, and more organized. It reduces manual work, saves time, avoids errors, and keeps all employee data safe and accessible anytime. Overall, this application provides a smooth and user-friendly experience for managing employees efficiently and paper-free.
